Submitting a Completion

Go to this google form: Submit a Completion

Fill out all required information. Your submission could take a little bit to be processed. Please keep in mind this is a community project and list mods will accept/reject submissions on thier own time. Prepare to have proof for a completion. You can also opt to do it live with a list moderator present.

Submitting a Level

Go to this google form: Submit a Level

Fill out all required information. Unlike completions, level submissions will most likely take some time to be processed. List Moderators will take community input into account when placing levels, so the process generally takes more time.

Requirements:

- Level must be at least 30 seconds long (medium length)
- Level must be verified, prepare to have proof. You can also opt to do it live with a list moderator present.
- Level must show effort put into it via decoration & gameplay

Impossible List Requirements
- Must be 30 seconds or longer and under 1 hour
- Level must be harder than the current Top 1 Ranked level at time of creation
- No more than 5 clicks in a frame
- Gameplay must be quality and physically possible in the current version of Geometry Dash

What List Moderators Do

List Moderators will judge the quality of level submissions and ensure that any verifications and completions are legitimate. Think of them like the "high council" of quality control.
