What's On This List?

This is a list ranking the hardest levels made by the Reesee Puffs discord. All levels are created and verified by members of the community, and the list is also maintained by members of the discord.

I know it's called the Reese Puffs Demon List but you level does not need to be at demon difficulty to be added to this list.

Ranking and Points

Levels are ranked based on their position on the list. The top 75 levels award points, with the number of points earned increasing as levels get harder.

You gain points by completing levels on the list that award points. However, you can
also earn points for verifying a level that is then placed on the list.
